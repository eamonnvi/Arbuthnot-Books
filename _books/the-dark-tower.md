**The Dark Tower** is a haunting, richly atmospheric historical novel set against the Anglo-Zulu War of 1879.

When Laura Brooke travels from England to South Africa with the mother of her lost fiancé, she expects a pilgrimage to a distant battlefield—and perhaps a final reckoning with grief. Instead, she is drawn into a world of heat, dust and sudden violence: colonial drawing rooms, military camps, treacherous roads and vast landscapes that seem to swallow both the living and the dead.

Told through the shifting perspectives of Laura, a young soldier, an ambitious war correspondent and Rejoice, a Zulu woman caught between worlds, the novel explores love, loyalty, memory and the uneasy truths behind imperial heroism. Christina Koning’s prose is vivid yet restrained, attentive to the small details of ordinary life as well as the terrible cost of war.

At its heart, **The Dark Tower** is a story of what people owe the dead—and what the living must do when the past refuses to remain buried.
