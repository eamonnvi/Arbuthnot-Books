A road between Hills Road and Mill Road in Cambridge. The stylishly comfortable house that [Grace Mitchell](/people/grace-mitchell/) shares with her husband [Peter Newman](/people/peter-newman/) is situated on this road. The location appears frequently in [Event/Horizon](/books/event-horizon/), [Palace of Tears](/books/palace-of-tears/) and [The Parallax View](/books/the-parallax-view/).
