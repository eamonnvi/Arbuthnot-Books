### Established identity and circumstances

Müller is an East German professor of German Romanticism, formerly associated with Humboldt University. He was once an SPD councillor in Pankow and later abandoned political opposition to preserve his academic career. He is exfiltrated from East Berlin using documents carried by Steve.

He claims that he and Mavis were lovers in 1948 and that they resumed their relationship more recently. The earlier relationship is supported by Mavis’s letter and the funeral account, but the recent relationship conflicts with the letters signed “S,” which were from Sheena.

### Desires and conflicts

Müller wants to preserve his intellectual work, especially his library, papers and Zettelkasten. He also wants to survive professionally and physically in the West. His account suggests regret over having prioritised academic advancement over political resistance.

His political loyalties remain uncertain. Sheena says the British intelligence service has reason to believe that he is still connected to the Stasi and may be intended to establish a network of DDR sympathisers in the British academy.

### Agency and relationships

Müller’s public funeral speech is a self-conscious performance. It praises Mavis but also centres his own history and reveals information that Collingwood says should have remained concealed. His private conversation with Steve is more candid but also strategically ambiguous.

He knows that Steve carried the documents to East Berlin and appears to know that Inge was the intermediary, contradicting some of the operational account previously given to Steve. This may indicate a breach in British procedure, Müller’s own intelligence access, or an intentional deception.

### Position at the end

Müller is moving to Cambridge as an extraordinary fellow of St Radegund’s and is connected with Grace’s academic future. He is simultaneously presented as a rescued victim, a compromised intellectual, a possible agent and a possible source of intelligence. No single interpretation is yet independently established.
