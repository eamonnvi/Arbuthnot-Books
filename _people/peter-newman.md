[Grace Mitchell's](/people/grace-mitchell/) husband. Born in Germany in 1930 to a Jewish family which had assimilated to Lutheranism. His father was a WWI war hero and judge. But the family had to flee to Britain when Peter was 9 years old. Former Slade student at the beginning of Event/Horizon he is a rising star of book publishing.
