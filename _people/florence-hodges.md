Flo owns The Croft, formerly a lodge connected to Wyvern Hall. She is Peter’s great-aunt, a former civil servant, and a wartime member of the Wrens. She has a gammy leg and is described as tall, imposing and still attractive (**“Puttenham Junction”**).

Flo is brisk, humorous, maternal and socially authoritative. She is rarely intimidated and often sees through other characters’ evasions.
